## 1. Description of the Service

Salah Lock is a mobile application developed for Android devices designed to encourage timely Islamic prayer (Salah) and reduce smartphone distraction. 

The App provides features including, but not limited to:
- **Prayer Time Calculations:** Calculating daily prayer windows (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, and Jumu'ah) based on device location and chosen calculation parameters.
- **App Interventions & Accountability:** Detecting user-designated distracting applications (“Red Zone” apps) and displaying a full-screen accountability prompt (“Have you prayed?”) during active prayer windows.
- **Strict Mode & Focus Controls:** Allowing users to configure focused lockouts and intentional unlocking rituals (such as Duas and reflection prompts).
- **Spiritual Habit Tracking:** Logging prayer punctuality, streaks, and focus metrics locally on your device.
- **Emergency Pause & Snooze:** Allowing temporary suspension of app interventions for urgent communication.

## 3. Device Permissions & System Requirements

To deliver its core distraction management features, Salah Lock requires specific Android system permissions granted voluntarily by you:

1. **Accessibility Service API (`BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE`):** Used solely to detect when a user-selected blocked application is in the foreground during an active prayer window. No text, screen content, passwords, or personal information is read, processed, or recorded.
2. **Display Over Other Apps (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`):** Enables the App to show the accountability overlay over restricted apps.
3. **Location Services (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` / `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`):** Required strictly for calculating astronomical prayer times based on geographic coordinates. Location data is processed locally.
4. **Exact Alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`) & Background Services:** Required to trigger prayer notifications and time-based overlays reliably.
5. **Installed Applications Query (`QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES`):** Allows you to select which installed applications you wish to restrict during prayer times.

You can modify or revoke these permissions at any time through your device's Android System Settings. Revoking required permissions may limit or disable the App's ability to function.
